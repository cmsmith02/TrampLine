This fun game is about a green blob named Christopher. He loves his trampolines and uses them as a form of transportation. He only has five trampolines so only five can be used at a time.  If he hits his head he will die because his blobby outside does not do a good job protecting his insides. He really wants to go to the pool so you better get him there! Good luck and have fun!
